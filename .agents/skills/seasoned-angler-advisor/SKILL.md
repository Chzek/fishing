# Seasoned Angler Advisor Skill

This skill provides domain expertise on angling practices, fish taxonomy, weather/water telemetry, tackle logistics, and real-world boat usability across Ontario waters and North American fisheries.

---

## 1. Core Domain Knowledge & Taxonomy

- **Fish Families & Species**:
  * **Percidae**: Walleye / Yellow Pickerel (*Sander vitreus*), Yellow Perch (*Perca flavescens*).
  * **Centrarchidae**: Smallmouth Bass (*Micropterus dolomieu*), Largemouth Bass (*Micropterus salmoides*), Black Crappie (*Pomoxis nigromaculatus*).
  * **Esocidae**: Northern Pike (*Esox lucius*), Muskellunge / Muskie (*Esox masquinongy*).
  * **Salmonidae**: Lake Trout (*Salvelinus namaycush*), Brook Trout (*Salvelinus fontinalis*), Rainbow Trout / Steelhead.
- **Tackle & Lure Categories**:
  * Crankbaits, Jerkbaits, Spinnerbaits, Soft Plastics (Swimbaits, Worms, Tubes), Jigs (Bucktail, Round-head), Topwater (Frogs, Poppers), Trolling Rigs (Downriggers, Dipsy Divers).

---

## 2. Real-World Boat Usability Standards

- **On-the-Water UX ("Quick Catch")**:
  * Anglers operate mobile devices on vibrating boats in direct sunlight with wet or gloved hands.
  * Inputs MUST be high-contrast, large touch targets ($\ge 48\text{px}$), with minimal required typing (prefer quick dropdowns, slider toggles, and GPS auto-detect).
- **Telemetry Correlations**:
  * Validate water telemetry fields (Water Temp °F/°C, Barometric Pressure in hPa/inHg, Wind Speed & Direction, Solunar Phases).
  * Ensure telemetry badges calculate realistic seasonal patterns (e.g., spring shallow bay walleye, summer deep structure trolling).
- **Fishery Regulations & Conservation**:
  * Maintain Ontario Fishery Management Zone (FMZ) exception rules, slot limits, sanctuaries, and Catch-and-Release rate telemetry.
